Proposed active definition: `https://id.i2idl.org/definitions/competency-framework-1`.

> A structured set of related competency definitions, organized by grouping, hierarchy or progression and often paired with proficiency levels or performance indicators, used as a shared reference to plan learning, assess performance and judge readiness.

This is a framework, distinct from an individual competency, the specification used to encode it, and a claim that a learner has demonstrated it. The primary field is Standards & Interoperability; secondary field memberships are Assessment & Measurement and Learning Engineering. It joins Framework and the existing Competency and Assessment curated collection. Eight associative relations are reciprocal. A narrower AI teacher framework hierarchy and a separate proficiency-level concept are deliberately left for editorial decision.
