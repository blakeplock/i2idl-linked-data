A shared language, published as a connected graph.

The I2IDL Glossary connects terminology across learning sciences, learning engineering, human-centered design, standards, analytics, and AI. Each concept has a stable identity and source-attributed definition, with relationships that can be followed across disciplinary boundaries.

The same curated data supports a human-readable glossary, semantic queries, visual exploration, and machine-to-machine integration.
