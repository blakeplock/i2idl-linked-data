Educational population

Definition

The people who participate in and support education systems, including learners, teachers, administrators, advisers, planners, support staff, and other educational personnel.

Editorial explanation

Education systems depend on multiple populations whose roles, needs, relationships, and participation shape how learning is delivered and experienced.
