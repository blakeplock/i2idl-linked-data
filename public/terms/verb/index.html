Standards & Interoperability / Concept

Verb

Definition

The xAPI element that identifies the action performed by an Actor. A Verb is identified by an IRI, while a language map can provide human-readable display text.

Editorial explanation

Verb identifiers carry the semantic meaning of what happened. Reusing stable Verb IRIs is central to making Statements understandable across systems.
