Education / Role

Classroom teacher

Definition

Professional teaching personnel primarily responsible for planning, organising, and conducting group learning activities that guide students' learning experiences.

Editorial explanation

The term distinguishes instructional personnel from aides and staff whose main responsibility is management or administration, which is essential for comparable staffing measures.
