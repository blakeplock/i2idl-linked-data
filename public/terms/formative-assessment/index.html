Assessment & Measurement / Practice

Formative assessment

Definition

Assessment used during learning to generate information that can guide improvement, feedback, instruction, or next steps rather than only certify a final outcome.

Editorial explanation

Continuous formative evidence can help learners and educators make adjustments while learning is still underway.
