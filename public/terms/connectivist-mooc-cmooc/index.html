Learning Sciences / Concept

Connectivist MOOC (cMOOC)

Definition

A massive open online course organized around connectivist principles, emphasizing networks, social learning, cooperation, and collaborative knowledge construction.

Editorial explanation

cMOOCs highlight a networked model in which learning emerges through connections among people, information, and communities.
