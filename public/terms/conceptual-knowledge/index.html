Conceptual knowledge

Definition

Knowledge of the ideas, relationships, and underlying reasons that explain why a procedure, principle, or solution works.

Editorial explanation

Conceptual knowledge helps learners move beyond memorized procedures and apply what they know in new or authentic contexts.
