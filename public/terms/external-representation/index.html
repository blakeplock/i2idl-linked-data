Human-Centered Design / Concept

External representation

Definition

A concrete artifact, such as a sketch, model, concept map, or prototype, that makes design thinking visible so it can support reflection, communication, collaboration, and iteration.

Editorial explanation

External representations move design knowledge out of an individual designer's head and give a team something persistent to examine, discuss, and revise.
