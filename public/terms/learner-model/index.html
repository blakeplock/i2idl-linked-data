Learner model

Definition

A representation of a learner's relevant characteristics, such as knowledge, skills, performance, preferences, goals, context, or other states used to inform instruction or personalization.

Editorial explanation

Learner models provide the information adaptive systems use to decide what support, content, sequence, or intervention may be appropriate next.
