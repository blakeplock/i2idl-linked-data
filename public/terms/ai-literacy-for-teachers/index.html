AI literacy for teachers

Definition

The foundational set of AI competencies teachers need to understand AI’s human-led nature, recognize benefits and risks, apply core ethical principles, understand basic AI concepts, and use appropriate AI tools in educational practice.

Editorial explanation

Teacher AI literacy provides the baseline needed for safe, ethical, and educationally useful engagement with AI before more advanced integration or creation.
