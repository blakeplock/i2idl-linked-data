Human accountability in AI

Definition

The principle that people remain responsible for decisions and outcomes in human–AI decision loops and that accountability should not be transferred to an AI system.

Editorial explanation

In education, human accountability preserves teachers’ legitimate responsibility for pedagogical decisions, tool selection, assessment, and student support.
