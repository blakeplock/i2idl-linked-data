Human-Centered Design / Discipline

Human-centered design

Definition

An approach to design that grounds decisions in an understanding of people, their goals, contexts, constraints, and experiences, using iterative research and testing.

Editorial explanation

Learning systems are more useful when learners and other stakeholders shape the problem definition as well as the solution.
