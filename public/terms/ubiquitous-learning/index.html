Learning Engineering / Concept

Ubiquitous learning

Definition

Learning understood as occurring across many places, times, devices, and contexts, including formal, nonformal, informal, workplace, and everyday settings.

Editorial explanation

Treating learning as ubiquitous changes what organizations choose to measure, recognize, connect, and support.
