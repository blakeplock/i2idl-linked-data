System of systems

Definition

A larger capability formed by multiple independent or semi-independent systems whose interactions create behaviors and outcomes at the enterprise level.

Editorial explanation

Learning ecosystems often involve content platforms, identity, data stores, analytics, credentials, HR systems, and human processes that must work together.
