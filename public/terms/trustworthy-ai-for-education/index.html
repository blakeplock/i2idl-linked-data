Trustworthy AI for education

Definition

AI used in education that is reliable and safe, robust against manipulation, protective of privacy and sensitive data, and appropriate to learners’ ages and pedagogical needs.

Editorial explanation

Educational institutions need confidence that AI systems will not expose learners to avoidable harm simply because a tool is technically available or commercially popular.
