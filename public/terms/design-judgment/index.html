Learning Engineering / Concept

Design judgment

Definition

The reasoning used to determine why a design choice is appropriate in a particular situation, drawing on knowledge, experience, theory, and contextual factors.

Editorial explanation

Design work rarely reduces to a fixed procedure. Judgment connects professional knowledge with the realities and constraints of a specific learning context.
