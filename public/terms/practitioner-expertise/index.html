Learning Engineering / Concept

Practitioner expertise

Definition

Knowledge and professional judgment developed through direct practice, including practical understanding of learners, settings, workflows, constraints, and implementation.

Editorial explanation

Practitioner expertise can reveal requirements and consequences that are difficult to see from research or technical development alone.
