Learning Engineering / Concept

Stakeholder requirement

Definition

A goal, requirement, recommendation, or idea expressed by a stakeholder that influences a design decision.

Editorial explanation

Stakeholders contribute different expertise and perspectives. Recording their requirements helps teams understand who or what shaped a particular decision.
