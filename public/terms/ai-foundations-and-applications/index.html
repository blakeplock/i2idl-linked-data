AI foundations and applications

Definition

The conceptual knowledge and transferable operational skills needed to understand how AI works and to evaluate, select, use, adapt, or customize AI tools for appropriate purposes.

Editorial explanation

AI competence requires more than operating a tool; it includes understanding technologies well enough to judge their capabilities, limitations, and appropriateness for context.
