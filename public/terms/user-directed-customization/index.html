Human-Centered Design / Practice

User-directed customization

Definition

The ability for users to adapt how a learning technology behaves or is configured to fit their needs, experience, context, or desired level of support.

Continue exploring

Editorial explanation

Customization can make a system useful across diverse learners and practitioners without assuming that one configuration or level of guidance will work for everyone.
