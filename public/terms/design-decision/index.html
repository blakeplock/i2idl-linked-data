Learning Engineering / Concept

Design decision

Definition

A choice about what to include in a learning design and how that choice will be enacted, made in response to goals, evidence, experience, constraints, and context.

Editorial explanation

Learning designs are built through many such choices. Making the choices visible helps teams explain, revisit, and improve the work over time.
