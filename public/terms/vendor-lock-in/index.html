Vendor lock-in

Definition

A condition in which earlier technology choices constrain future decisions because systems, data, or services are difficult to move, replace, or integrate.

Editorial explanation

Open standards and interoperability can reduce dependence on a single technology provider and preserve future choices for education systems.
