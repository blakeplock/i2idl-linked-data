Learning Sciences / Concept

Sensemaking

Definition

The social and cognitive process of interpreting complex or ambiguous information by connecting observations, experiences, narratives, and shared context.

Editorial explanation

In social learning environments, sensemaking helps groups turn distributed information into shared understanding and action.
