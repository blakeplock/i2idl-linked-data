Acquire (AI competency level)

Definition

The foundational AI competency level in which teachers develop the essential knowledge, ethical awareness, and practical skills needed to evaluate, select, and use AI tools appropriately.

Editorial explanation

Acquire establishes basic AI literacy and a foundation for more advanced integration of AI into educational practice.
