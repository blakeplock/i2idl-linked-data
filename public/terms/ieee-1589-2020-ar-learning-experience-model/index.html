IEEE 1589-2020 — Augmented Reality Learning Experience Model

Definition

An IEEE standard for interoperable augmented-reality learning systems. It defines a conceptual model for relationships among the physical world, the learner, digital information, learning activities, and the environment, along with data models and XML/JSON bindings for representing activities and context.

Editorial explanation

A common AR learning model supports reusable content, interchangeable components, experience repositories, and learning experiences that can move across otherwise proprietary AR platforms.
