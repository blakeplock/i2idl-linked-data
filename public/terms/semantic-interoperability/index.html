Semantic interoperability

Definition

The ability of independently developed systems to exchange data in ways that preserve shared meaning, so equivalent concepts and activity descriptions can be interpreted consistently across implementations.

Editorial explanation

xAPI Profiles were created in part to reduce inconsistent expressions of the same human activity and to give communities a common way to define and reuse meaning across systems.
