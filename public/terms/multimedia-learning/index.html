Learning Sciences / Concept

Multimedia learning

Definition

Learning that uses more than one mode of information presentation, such as combining words, narration, graphics, animation, or other media within an instructional environment.

Editorial explanation

Multimedia learning theory links design choices to cognitive limits and helps designers decide how to combine visual and auditory information.
