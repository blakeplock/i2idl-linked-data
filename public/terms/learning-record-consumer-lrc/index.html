Learning Record Consumer (LRC)

Definition

An xAPI Client that retrieves Learning Records from one or more Learning Record Stores for processing such as interpretation, analysis, translation, dissemination, or aggregation.

Editorial explanation

xAPI is not only about sending data into an LRS. LRCs represent the consuming side of the ecosystem, where records are turned into analytics, services, reports, or other downstream uses.
