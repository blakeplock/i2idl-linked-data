Education data ecosystem

Definition

The interconnected set of data sources, production and reporting processes, analytical practices, institutions, and uses that together support evidence-based decision-making in an education system.

Editorial explanation

Viewing education data as an ecosystem shifts attention from isolated datasets to the relationships among production, sharing, analysis, governance, and policy use.
