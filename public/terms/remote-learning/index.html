Learning Engineering / Concept

Remote learning

Definition

Learning in which learners and teachers may be separated by time and/or location and interact through computers or telecommunications technologies.

Editorial explanation

Remote learning changes the conditions under which learners access instruction, interact with teachers and peers, manage time, and use digital resources.
