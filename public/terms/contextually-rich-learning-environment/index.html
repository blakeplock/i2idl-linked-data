Human-Centered Design / Concept

Contextually rich learning environment

Definition

A learning environment understood and designed in relation to the specific people, practices, technologies, constraints, and settings in which learning occurs.

Editorial explanation

A design that works in one setting may fail in another, so learning technologies often need to account for local context rather than assume a single common environment.
