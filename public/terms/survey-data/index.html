Data & Instrumentation / Concept

Survey data

Definition

Education-relevant data collected through instruments such as household surveys, labour-force surveys, and population censuses, often providing information not captured in administrative systems.

Editorial explanation

Survey data can reveal access, participation, attainment, skills, household conditions, and equity dimensions and can complement or validate administrative records.
