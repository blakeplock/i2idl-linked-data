Internationalized Resource Identifier (IRI)

Definition

A globally unique identifier format used throughout xAPI to identify things such as Verbs, Activities, Activity types, extensions, and other vocabulary concepts. IRIs can contain international characters and include a scheme.

Editorial explanation

IRIs let independent systems refer to the same concepts without relying on local database identifiers, which is fundamental to semantic interoperability.
