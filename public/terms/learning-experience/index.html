Standards & Interoperability / Concept

Learning experience

Definition

An event associated with learning, broadly interpreted to include formal and informal experiences such as courses, reading, field experiences, self-directed work, or receiving a certificate.

Editorial explanation

xAPI is intentionally designed to track learning beyond a single delivery system or course, so the broad meaning of “learning experience” sets the scope for what can be recorded.
