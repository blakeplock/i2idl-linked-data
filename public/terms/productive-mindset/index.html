Learning Sciences / Concept

Productive mindset

Definition

A pattern of beliefs and attitudes that supports effort, persistence, strategy use, and engagement with learning challenges.

Editorial explanation

Learning environments can influence whether difficulty is interpreted as useful effort, lack of ability, or a signal to disengage.
