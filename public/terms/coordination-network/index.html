Learning Engineering / System

Coordination network

Definition

A sustained network or coordinating structure that connects participants, brokers relationships, supports collaborative work, synthesizes outcomes, and helps knowledge move across projects and communities.

Editorial explanation

Coordination networks can preserve relationships and shared learning beyond individual projects while helping a field connect research, practice, and emerging areas of expertise.
