Assessment & Measurement / Method

Cross-national assessment

Definition

A regional or international learning assessment that uses a common agreed framework and similar procedures across participating systems to produce comparable learning-outcome data.

Editorial explanation

Cross-national assessments support comparison across education systems and can provide contextual information about students, teachers, schools, and equity.
