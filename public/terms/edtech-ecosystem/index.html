EdTech ecosystem

Definition

The network of public, private, academic, civil-society, donor, school, family, and learner stakeholders involved in developing, providing, governing, and using education technology.

Editorial explanation

EdTech implementation depends on relationships among many actors, which makes ecosystem coordination a core systems and governance concern.
