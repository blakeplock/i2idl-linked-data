Metadata Provider

Definition

A person, organization, software program, or other entity that creates IRIs for xAPI use or hosts metadata describing those IRIs.

Editorial explanation

The stability and clarity of xAPI vocabularies depend on parties that control identifiers and make their intended meaning available to others.
