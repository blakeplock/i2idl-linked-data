Learning Sciences / Concept

Learner variability

Definition

Differences among learners in needs, backgrounds, abilities, contexts, experiences, and ways of engaging with or using a learning system.

Editorial explanation

Designing for variability shifts attention away from an assumed average learner and toward technologies and experiences that can work across diverse people and settings.
