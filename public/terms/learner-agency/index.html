Learning Sciences / Concept

Learner agency

Definition

The capacity and opportunity for learners to make choices, exercise control, and take an active role in directing aspects of their own learning.

Editorial explanation

Agency supports active participation and can help learners connect effort, strategy, choice, and responsibility to their progress.
