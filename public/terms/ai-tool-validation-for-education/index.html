AI tool validation for education

Definition

The systematic evaluation of an AI system or tool before educational adoption to determine whether it is sufficiently reliable, trustworthy, safe, ethically compliant, age-appropriate, and pedagogically suitable.

Editorial explanation

Validation creates an institutional checkpoint between the availability of an AI product and its use with learners or educators at scale.
