Digital global public goods

Definition

Reusable digital resources intended for broad public benefit, including research, digital resources and applications, databases, repositories, standards, algorithms, and platforms that can be adapted across contexts.

Editorial explanation

Digital global public goods can reduce duplicated effort and give education systems a shared base that can be adapted to local needs.
