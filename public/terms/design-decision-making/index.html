Learning Engineering / Practice

Design decision-making

Definition

The process of forming, enacting, communicating, revisiting, and sometimes revising choices about a learning design while accounting for learners, stakeholders, evidence, technology, and context.

Editorial explanation

Tracking decision-making makes design reasoning more transparent and supports iteration across people, project phases, and learning engineering cycles.
