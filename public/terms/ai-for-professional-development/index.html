AI for professional development

Definition

The use of AI to support teachers’ lifelong professional learning, collaborative development, reflection, assessment of learning needs, and transformation of professional practice.

Editorial explanation

AI can support professional growth when educators retain control over goals, judgement, collaboration, and the interpretation of recommendations or analytics.
