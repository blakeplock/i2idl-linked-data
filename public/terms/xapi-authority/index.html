xAPI authority

Definition

The Agent or Group represented in a Statement as asserting that the Statement is true. The LRS verifies or sets the authority based on authentication and trust relationships.

Editorial explanation

Authority distinguishes who performed an experience from who vouches for the resulting record, which is important for provenance and trust.
