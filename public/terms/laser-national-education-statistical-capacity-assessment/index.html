LASER

Definition

A UNESCO Institute for Statistics assessment framework for evaluating and strengthening national education data ecosystems across learning assessments, administrative data, survey systems, expenditure data, and review and monitoring.

Editorial explanation

LASER provides a structured approach for identifying gaps in education data quality, coverage, reporting, and alignment with standards and for prioritizing improvements.
