Learning Engineering / Concept

Contextual factor

Definition

A condition surrounding a design, such as learner needs, curriculum structure, timeline, resources, or technology, that can influence what is feasible or appropriate.

Editorial explanation

Learning design is situated. Contextual factors can change over time and may cause teams to revisit decisions that were reasonable under earlier conditions.
