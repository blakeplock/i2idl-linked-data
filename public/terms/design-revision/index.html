Learning Engineering / Practice

Design revision

Definition

A change to something previously decided or to an existing element of a learning design.

Continue exploring

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Editorial explanation

Revisions make iteration visible. Tracking them connects changes to the evidence, experience, requirements, technology, or context that prompted reconsideration.
