Early childhood education (ECE)

Definition

The educational component of early childhood education and care, comprising ISCED level 0 programmes with organised and intentional learning activities that support children's cognitive, physical, social, and emotional development.

Editorial explanation

ECE distinguishes formally classified educational programmes from the broader mix of education and care services included within ECEC.
