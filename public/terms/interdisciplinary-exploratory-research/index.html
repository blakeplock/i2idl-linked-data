Learning Engineering / Practice

Interdisciplinary exploratory research

Definition

Exploratory research that brings together expertise, methods, and perspectives from multiple disciplines to investigate emerging questions and develop novel approaches.

Editorial explanation

Learning technology problems often cross technical, educational, accessibility, and domain boundaries, so discovery can depend on collaboration that no single discipline can supply.
