Education / Concept

Education

Definition

The process through which people develop abilities, attitudes, knowledge, and forms of behavior that are valued within the society in which they live.

Editorial explanation

Education provides the broad frame for how people develop knowledge, skills, capabilities, values, and behaviors across formal, nonformal, and informal settings.
