Learning Sciences / Practice

AI-enhanced pedagogical innovation

Definition

The critical and exploratory use of AI to develop or transform learning scenarios, pedagogical approaches, assessment, and student-centred learning while maintaining human-centred educational principles.

Editorial explanation

At an advanced level, AI can become part of redesigning learning rather than merely automating existing activities, but innovation still requires human judgement and evidence.
