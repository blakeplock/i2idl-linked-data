User-tailored privacy

Definition

A privacy approach in which a system models an individual learner's privacy concerns and adapts privacy information, recommendations, or decision support to that user.

Editorial explanation

It seeks a middle ground between giving learners no meaningful control and overwhelming them with too many settings and notices.
