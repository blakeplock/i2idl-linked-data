Create (AI competency level)

Definition

The advanced AI competency level in which teachers critically and creatively configure, adapt, or co-create AI-enabled approaches, tools, policies, and pedagogical practices for educational transformation.

Editorial explanation

Create emphasizes expert judgement, innovation, ethical co-creation, and adaptation to local educational needs rather than simple tool proficiency.
