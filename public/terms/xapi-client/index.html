xAPI Client

Definition

Any entity that interacts with xAPI services through requests, including Learning Record Providers, Learning Record Consumers, Learning Record Stores, or other systems.

Editorial explanation

The Client concept makes clear that xAPI communication is not limited to one application type; several kinds of systems can participate in the exchange of learning data.
