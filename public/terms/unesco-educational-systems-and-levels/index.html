Education / System

Educational systems and levels

Definition

The organized structures through which formal and nonformal education is provided, including the institutions, programs, pathways, and successive stages or levels that make up an education system.

Editorial explanation

This concept provides a way to describe how education is organized across institutions and across stages such as early childhood, primary, secondary, and higher education.
