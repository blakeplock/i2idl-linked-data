Pattern validation

Definition

The process of validating a series of Statements that share a registration, and when applicable a subregistration, to determine whether they follow a specified Profile Pattern.

Editorial explanation

Pattern validation makes it possible to validate multi-Statement workflows and activity sequences rather than evaluating each Statement only in isolation.
