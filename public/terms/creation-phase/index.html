Learning Engineering / Practice

Creation phase

Definition

The part of the learning engineering process in which a team creates a response to a learning challenge by designing the solution, planning and instrumenting the needed data, and iterating the design with learners and stakeholders.

Editorial explanation

Treating instrumentation and iteration as part of creation helps ensure that a solution is designed to generate useful evidence from the start.
