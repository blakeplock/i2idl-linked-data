Learning Engineering / Concept

Learning engineering team

Definition

The person or interdisciplinary group assembled to address a learning challenge, with the mix of skills and knowledge determined by the problem, context, learners, stakeholders, and technical needs.

Editorial explanation

Learning engineering problems often cross disciplinary boundaries, so team composition should follow the challenge rather than a fixed job-title template.
