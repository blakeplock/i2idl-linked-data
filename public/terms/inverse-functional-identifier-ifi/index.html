Inverse Functional Identifier (IFI)

Definition

An identifier used by xAPI to uniquely identify a particular Agent persona or Group, such as a mailbox, mailbox hash, OpenID, or account.

Editorial explanation

IFIs are the basis for determining when Agent or Group representations refer to the same identity, which affects querying, deduplication, and record continuity.
