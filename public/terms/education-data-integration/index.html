Education data integration

Definition

The combination and linkage of data from different education and related sources to create a more coherent view, fill information gaps, and support analysis that a single source cannot provide.

Editorial explanation

Integrated data can connect administrative records, assessments, surveys, finance, and other sources to answer policy questions that span institutional and data boundaries.
