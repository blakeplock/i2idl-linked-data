Education data cycle

Definition

A recurring sequence in which education data is produced and made available, reported and shared, analyzed, and then used to inform decisions and further data needs.

Editorial explanation

The cycle emphasizes that collecting data is only one stage of an effective data system; value emerges when data moves through reporting, analysis, and use.
