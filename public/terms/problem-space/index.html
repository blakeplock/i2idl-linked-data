Learning Engineering / Concept

Problem space

Definition

The contextual setting in which a learning challenge exists, including the conditions, constraints, learners, stakeholders, systems, and uncertainties that shape possible solutions.

Editorial explanation

Learning engineering may begin in either well-defined or ill-defined situations; making the problem space explicit helps teams avoid designing a solution for the wrong context.
