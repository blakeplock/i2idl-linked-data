Human-centred mindset

Definition

A values- and attitudes-based orientation toward human–AI interaction that keeps human rights, human flourishing, human agency, and human accountability central to the use of AI.

Editorial explanation

A human-centred mindset helps educators evaluate AI in terms of its consequences for people, learning, society, and citizenship rather than only its technical performance.
