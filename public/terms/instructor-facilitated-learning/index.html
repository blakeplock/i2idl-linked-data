Learning Engineering / Concept

Instructor-facilitated learning

Definition

Learning in which an instructor actively guides, structures, supports, or responds to learners as they engage with learning activities.

Editorial explanation

Instructor facilitation can provide timely guidance and social interaction, but instructor time is a limited resource that should be allocated where it most supports learning.
