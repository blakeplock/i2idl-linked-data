Endpoint

Definition

An entry point used for communication with a service. In xAPI, resource locations are exposed through IRIs or URLs rooted at an LRS endpoint.

Editorial explanation

Endpoints define where interoperable systems send and retrieve records and related resources, separating the communication contract from a specific product implementation.
