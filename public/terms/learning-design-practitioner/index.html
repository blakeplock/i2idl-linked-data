Learning Engineering / Discipline

Learning design practitioner (LDP)

Definition

An umbrella term for practitioners who design learning resources or experiences, including roles such as instructional designer, learning engineer, and learning technologist.

Editorial explanation

The term makes it possible to discuss design practice across job titles while focusing on the shared work of shaping learning experiences.
