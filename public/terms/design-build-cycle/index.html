Learning Engineering / Method

Design-build cycle

Definition

An iterative cycle in which a learning solution is designed, built, tried with learners or stakeholders, examined through evidence, and revised rather than treated as complete after a single design pass.

Editorial explanation

Repeated design-build cycles make learner and stakeholder feedback part of development and support faster correction of weak assumptions.
