Learning Engineering / Concept

Emergent impact

Definition

Effects, uses, outcomes, or extensions of exploratory work that develop beyond what was specified or anticipated at the start of a project.

Editorial explanation

Exploratory work may create value through unexpected applications, new research directions, broader reach, or later stages of development that could not be predicted in the original proposal.
