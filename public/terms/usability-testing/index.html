Usability testing

Definition

Evaluation in which representative users attempt tasks so a team can observe where an interface or experience succeeds, confuses, or creates friction.

Editorial explanation

Usability affects whether learners and staff can access the intended learning experience and whether data-generating workflows work in practice.
