Education statistical capacity

Definition

The capability of an education system to produce, report, assess, and use education data of sufficient coverage and quality to generate indicators and support policy and governance.

Editorial explanation

Statistical capacity determines whether an education system can reliably monitor itself, meet national information needs, and contribute comparable data for international monitoring.
