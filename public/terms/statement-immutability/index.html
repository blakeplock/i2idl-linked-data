Statement immutability

Definition

The xAPI principle that key semantic parts of a Statement are not changed after the Statement has been stored. The standard identifies specific properties that are immutable and others that may differ without changing the Statement’s essential meaning.

Editorial explanation

Immutability allows Statements to be shared between LRSs while preserving the evidence they represent and reduces ambiguity about whether two copies describe the same event.
