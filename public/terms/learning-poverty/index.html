Assessment & Measurement / Concept

Learning poverty

Definition

A measure of the share of 10-year-old children who cannot read and understand a simple story.

Editorial explanation

The measure provides a system-level indicator of foundational learning challenges and is used in the source to frame the broader learning crisis.
