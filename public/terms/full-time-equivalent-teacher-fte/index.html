Full-time equivalent teacher (FTE)

Definition

A standardised measure of teacher workload relative to full-time employment, expressed against the normal statutory working hours for the reference period.

Editorial explanation

FTE measures make staffing levels comparable when teachers work different schedules and allow ratios and workforce indicators to use a common workload unit.
