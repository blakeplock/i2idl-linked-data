Meta-adaptation

Definition

Adaptation across multiple learning systems or resources, where one system can use shared data to recommend or coordinate interventions beyond its own immediate instructional environment.

Editorial explanation

Meta-adaptation is a property of connected learning ecosystems and depends on interoperable data, standardized protocols, and well-defined metadata.
