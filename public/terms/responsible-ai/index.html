Responsible AI

Definition

The design, use, and governance of AI systems with attention to evidence, safety, privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, and human consequences.

Editorial explanation

Learning contexts can affect opportunities and records about people, so AI decisions require more than technical performance alone.
