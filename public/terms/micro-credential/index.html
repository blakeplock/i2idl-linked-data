Micro-credential

Definition

A credential representing demonstrated achievement in a smaller unit of learning or competency than a traditional degree or major qualification.

Editorial explanation

Micro-credentials can make specific capabilities visible and portable, especially when tied to clear competency definitions and verifiable evidence.
