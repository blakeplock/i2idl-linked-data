Standards & Interoperability / Process

Statement Template validation

Definition

The process of validating a Statement against a Profile’s Statement Templates to determine which templates match and whether the matching templates’ rules are satisfied.

Editorial explanation

Template validation provides a repeatable way to verify that Statement structure follows the interoperability rules expressed by a Profile.
