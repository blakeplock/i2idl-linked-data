Education expenditure data

Definition

Financial data describing income and spending related to education from public, private, household, and other sources, including spending on infrastructure, personnel, and other education costs.

Editorial explanation

Expenditure data connects resource allocation with education provision and outcomes and is necessary for monitoring the financial dimension of education systems.
