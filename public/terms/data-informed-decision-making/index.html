Learning Engineering / Practice

Data-informed decision making

Definition

A decision process that uses relevant data as evidence while also considering context, expertise, goals, and constraints.

Editorial explanation

Data can improve decisions without pretending that metrics alone contain the full meaning of a learning situation.
