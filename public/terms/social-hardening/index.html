Social hardening

Definition

The use of training, exercises, and organizational learning to strengthen people's ability to recognize, prevent, contain, and respond to security threats.

Editorial explanation

Technical controls alone are insufficient when human behavior is part of the security system; resilience also depends on people understanding how and why controls work.
