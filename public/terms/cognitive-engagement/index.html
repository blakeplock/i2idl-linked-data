Learning Sciences / Concept

Cognitive engagement

Definition

The degree to which a learner actively thinks about, applies, analyzes, and connects knowledge rather than only receiving or memorizing information.

Editorial explanation

Deeper cognitive engagement supports stronger understanding and more flexible application of knowledge and skills.
