Education technology (EdTech)

Definition

The use of hardware, software, digital content, data, and information systems in education to support teaching, learning, education management, and delivery.

Editorial explanation

EdTech is a broad systems concept that includes far more than devices. It connects instructional practice, content, data, infrastructure, and education management.
