Education / Concept

Childcare gap

Definition

The period between the end of paid parental leave and the age at which children become legally entitled to access early childhood education and care services.

Editorial explanation

The childcare gap makes continuity between family leave policy and guaranteed early childhood provision measurable across education and social-policy systems.
