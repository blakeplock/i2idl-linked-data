Learning Engineering / Concept

Asynchronous learning

Definition

Learning activity that can occur at different times, allowing learners to engage with materials or tasks without everyone participating simultaneously.

Editorial explanation

Asynchronous formats provide flexibility and independent pacing, but they require careful design for guidance, interaction, and learner self-management.
