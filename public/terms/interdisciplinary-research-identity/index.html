Learning Engineering / Concept

Interdisciplinary research identity

Definition

A professional identity in which researchers understand their work as belonging across disciplinary boundaries and participate in collaborations, methods, and communities that span established fields.

Editorial explanation

A durable interdisciplinary identity can support professional growth and participation in research problems that do not fit neatly within one established discipline.
