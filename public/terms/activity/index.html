Standards & Interoperability / Concept

Activity

Definition

In xAPI, an Activity is something an Actor interacts with or experiences and that can serve as the object of a tracked Statement. Activities can represent instruction, performance, experiences, resources, events, or even physical or virtual objects.

Editorial explanation

Activities are the primary way xAPI identifies what an experience was about, allowing the same model to describe learning across courses, simulations, work, media, and real-world events.
