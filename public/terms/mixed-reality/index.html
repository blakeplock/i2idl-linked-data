Mixed reality

Definition

A continuum of immersive technologies that includes virtual reality and augmented reality, combining physical and digital experience to varying degrees.

Editorial explanation

Mixed reality can support situated practice, simulation, and experiential learning that would be difficult, dangerous, or costly to reproduce physically.
