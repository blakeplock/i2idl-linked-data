Learning Sciences / Practice

AI–pedagogy integration

Definition

The purposeful integration of AI into student-centred learning design and facilitation to support engagement, differentiated learning, teacher–student interaction, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

Editorial explanation

Integration requires aligning AI capabilities with pedagogical principles and learning goals instead of treating AI as an add-on to existing instruction.
