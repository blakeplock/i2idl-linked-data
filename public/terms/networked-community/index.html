Learning Engineering / Concept

Networked community

Definition

A connected community of researchers, practitioners, and other participants that shares expertise, builds relationships, supports collaboration, and extends work across projects and disciplines.

Editorial explanation

Networked communities can add capacity to individual projects by connecting people to expertise, mentorship, implementation knowledge, and opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration.
