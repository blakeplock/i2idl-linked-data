Ethics of AI

Definition

The body of ethical values, principles, regulations, institutional rules, and practical guidance used to evaluate and govern the design and use of artificial intelligence.

Editorial explanation

For educators, AI ethics connects abstract principles to decisions about tools, data, human rights, inclusion, safety, and responsible educational practice.
