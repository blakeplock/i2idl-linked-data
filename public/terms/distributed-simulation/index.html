Distributed simulation

Definition

Simulation conducted across networked systems so multiple participants or simulated entities can interact within a shared scenario or synthetic environment.

Editorial explanation

Distributed simulation supports collective and team-based training and helped drive interoperability standards such as DIS and HLA.
