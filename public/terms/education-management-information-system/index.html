Education Management Information System

Definition

An information system used by education authorities to collect, process, analyze, monitor, and disseminate administrative data for education management and planning.

Editorial explanation

An EMIS is a core infrastructure component for national education data, supporting routine administration, indicator production, planning, and integration with other data sources.
