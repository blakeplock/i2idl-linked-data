Safe and responsible use of AI

Definition

The use of AI in ways that apply ethical rules, protect privacy and intellectual property, follow applicable legal requirements, mitigate bias and harm, and preserve human responsibility.

Editorial explanation

Responsible educational use depends on both the characteristics of the AI system and the decisions made by the people and institutions using it.
