Educational sciences and environment

Definition

The interdisciplinary study of education and the environments in which teaching and learning occur, including pedagogical, psychological, social, historical, philosophical, cultural, and contextual influences.

Editorial explanation

It connects the study of learning and education with the human and environmental conditions that shape educational practice and outcomes.
