AI competency progression

Definition

A scaffolded reference pathway for developing AI competency across increasing levels of capability without assuming that development is strictly linear or identical across competency areas.

Editorial explanation

Progression models help organizations plan development and assess growth while recognizing that people can be more advanced in some aspects of AI competency than others.
