Voided Statement

Definition

A previously issued xAPI Statement that has been marked invalid through a separate voiding Statement that targets it by Statement Reference.

Editorial explanation

Voiding preserves the historical record while providing a standards-defined way to indicate that an earlier Statement should no longer be treated as valid evidence.
