Learning Engineering / System

Learning Engineering Evidence and Decision (LEED) tracker

Definition

A learning-engineering documentation structure for recording design decisions, related justifications, sources of influence, and revisions in connection with the learning engineering process.

Editorial explanation

The tracker externalizes design reasoning so teams can communicate, revisit decisions, support iteration, and study authentic design work without relying only on retrospective recall.
