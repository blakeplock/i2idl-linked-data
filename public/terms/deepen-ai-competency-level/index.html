Deepen (AI competency level)

Definition

The intermediate AI competency level in which teachers integrate AI into educational practice with greater attention to human accountability, safe and responsible use, ethical evaluation, and pedagogical design.

Editorial explanation

Deepen moves beyond basic literacy toward sustained, context-aware use of AI in teaching, learning, and professional practice.
