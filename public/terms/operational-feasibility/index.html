Operational feasibility

Definition

The degree to which a proposed learning solution can be implemented, supported, governed, maintained, and sustained in its real operating environment.

Editorial explanation

A solution that works in a pilot can still fail when staffing, process, integration, cost, or administrative constraints are ignored.
