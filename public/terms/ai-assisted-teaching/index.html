Learning Sciences / Practice

AI-assisted teaching

Definition

Teaching in which educators identify and use appropriate AI tools to support activities such as lesson planning, instruction, assessment, and learner support while mitigating associated risks.

Editorial explanation

AI-assisted teaching frames AI as a support to professional practice and requires educators to connect tool use to specific pedagogical needs.
