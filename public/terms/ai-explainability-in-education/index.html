AI explainability in education

Definition

The extent to which educators can understand and critically examine how an AI system reaches outputs or conclusions sufficiently to assess its use and intervene when necessary.

Editorial explanation

Explainability supports informed human oversight, particularly when AI outputs influence teaching, learning, assessment, or decisions about learners.
