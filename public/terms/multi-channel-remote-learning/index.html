Multi-channel remote learning

Definition

Remote learning delivered through a combination of channels such as radio, television, mobile devices, and online platforms in order to reach learners with different levels of access.

Editorial explanation

A multi-channel approach can reduce dependence on any single form of connectivity and broaden access to learning outside school.
