Human-Centered Design / Method

Co-design

Definition

A collaborative design approach in which researchers or designers work with practitioners, learners, partners, or other affected participants to shape questions, technologies, and practices.

Editorial explanation

Co-design brings practical knowledge and user perspectives into the design process early enough to change the direction of the work rather than merely evaluate a finished solution.
