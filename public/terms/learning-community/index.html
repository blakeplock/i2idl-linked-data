Learning Sciences / Concept

Learning community

Definition

A group of learners who work together on problems or learning goals within physical or virtual spaces supported by shared norms, processes, and scaffolds.

Editorial explanation

Learning communities provide social structures in which learners can discuss, apply, and construct knowledge together.
