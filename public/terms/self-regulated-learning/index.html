Learning Sciences / Concept

Self-regulated learning

Definition

Learning in which individuals plan, monitor, evaluate, and adjust their goals, strategies, effort, and behavior.

Editorial explanation

Well-designed learning systems can help learners become active managers of their own progress rather than passive recipients of instruction.
