Learning Engineering / System

Massive open online course (MOOC)

Definition

An online course designed for open access and participation at large scale, often serving learners across institutions and geographic boundaries.

Editorial explanation

MOOCs demonstrated how cloud platforms and online course models could reach very large populations while experimenting with new approaches to access and scale.
