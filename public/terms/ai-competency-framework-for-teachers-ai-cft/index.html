AI competency framework for teachers (AI CFT)

Definition

A reference framework describing the knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes teachers need to use artificial intelligence safely, effectively, ethically, and in ways that preserve human-centred education.

Editorial explanation

An AI competency framework provides a structured basis for teacher preparation, professional learning, competency assessment, and the development of national or institutional expectations for AI use in education.
