Learning Engineering / Practice

Microlearning

Definition

Learning delivered in small, focused units that are often self-paced, flexible, and designed for use near the moment of need.

Editorial explanation

Microlearning is useful when learners need targeted refreshers or short learning episodes that fit into work and daily activity.
