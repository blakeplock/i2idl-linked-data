Affective computing

Definition

A field of computing concerned with recognizing, modeling, or responding to human emotional states and, in some cases, simulating affective behavior in machines.

Editorial explanation

In learning systems, affective signals can inform adaptive responses to states such as boredom, frustration, engagement, or confidence.
