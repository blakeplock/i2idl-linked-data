Learning ecosystem governance

Definition

The structures and processes used to coordinate authorities, policies, resources, standards, support, experimentation, and decision making across a learning ecosystem.

Editorial explanation

Connected ecosystems span many stakeholders and systems, so they need explicit mechanisms for shared decisions, local autonomy, policy enforcement, resourcing, and change.
