Human-Centered Design / Practice

Equitable co-design

Definition

Co-design that deliberately lowers barriers to participation, includes underrepresented perspectives, accounts for intersecting identities and needs, and gives practitioners or users meaningful influence over design decisions.

Editorial explanation

Equitable co-design treats participation, accessibility, autonomy, and representation as design conditions rather than as checks performed after a technology has been built.
