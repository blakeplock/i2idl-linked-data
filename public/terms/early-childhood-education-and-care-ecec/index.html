Early childhood education and care (ECEC)

Definition

Services and programmes that support the education, development, well-being, and care of young children before primary education, including ISCED level 0 programmes and regulated childcare outside the formal education system.

Editorial explanation

ECEC names the broader early-years service landscape and distinguishes it from the narrower category of programmes classified as formal early childhood education.
