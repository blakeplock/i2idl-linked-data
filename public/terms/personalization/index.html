Learning Engineering / Concept

Personalization

Definition

The adjustment of learning content, sequencing, support, interface, or goals to characteristics or needs associated with an individual learner.

Editorial explanation

Personalization can improve relevance, but it requires clear objectives, trustworthy evidence, and safeguards against opaque or unjustified decisions.
