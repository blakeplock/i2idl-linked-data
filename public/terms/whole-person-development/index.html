Learning Sciences / Concept

Whole-person development

Definition

An approach to development that treats cognitive, social, emotional, and physical capabilities as interconnected parts of learning across a person's life.

Editorial explanation

Whole-person development broadens learning goals beyond academic knowledge and recognizes capabilities such as resilience, self-regulation, communication, and physical readiness.
