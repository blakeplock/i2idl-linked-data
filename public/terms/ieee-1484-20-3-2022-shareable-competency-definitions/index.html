IEEE 1484.20.3-2022 — Shareable Competency Definitions

Definition

An IEEE standard defining a data model for describing, referencing, and sharing competency definitions, competency frameworks, and rubrics across learning, credentialing, workforce, and employment systems.

Editorial explanation

Common competency identifiers and structures let different systems refer to the same skills, knowledge, abilities, behaviors, and learning outcomes with consistent meaning.
