Education / Concept

Personalized learning

Definition

Learning designed around the needs of an individual student and intended to provide appropriate learning opportunities across school and home contexts.

Editorial explanation

The World Bank roadmap treats personalization as a design consideration for aligning EdTech with learner needs rather than simply increasing technology use.
