Learning Engineering / Concept

Future learning ecosystem

Definition

A vision of learning as a connected, lifelong continuum rather than a series of disconnected episodes, with experiences tailored to individuals across locations, media, organizations, and periods of time.

Editorial explanation

It provides the organizing idea for linking learning science, technology, data, governance, policy, and human infrastructure into one evolving system.
