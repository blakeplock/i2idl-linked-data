Scalability

Definition

The ability of a learning solution, system, or process to handle growth in users, data, complexity, scope, or demand without unacceptable degradation.

Editorial explanation

Scaling changes architecture, support, cost, governance, and coordination requirements even when the learning design itself appears stable.
