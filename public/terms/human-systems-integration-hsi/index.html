Human-Systems Integration (HSI)

Definition

A philosophy and set of processes for addressing human performance at the system level throughout design, development, operation, and the broader system lifecycle.

Editorial explanation

HSI helps multidisciplinary teams treat people as integral system components and balance human needs with technology, organizational constraints, cost, and lifecycle risk.
