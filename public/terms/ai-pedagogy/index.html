Learning Sciences / Practice

AI pedagogy

Definition

The competencies and practices involved in selecting, validating, and integrating AI with pedagogical methods to support teaching, learning, social interaction, care, and assessment.

Editorial explanation

AI pedagogy focuses attention on when and how AI should support educational practice rather than assuming that the presence of AI creates pedagogical value.
