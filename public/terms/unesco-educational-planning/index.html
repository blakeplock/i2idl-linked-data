Educational planning

Definition

The systematic process of analyzing educational needs, setting goals and priorities, anticipating future requirements, and organizing resources and actions to improve an education system.

Editorial explanation

Planning connects educational goals to the people, resources, timelines, data, and institutional changes needed to achieve them.
