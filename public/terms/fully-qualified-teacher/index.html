Education / Role

Fully qualified teacher

Definition

A teacher who has satisfied all training and administrative requirements for teaching a specified grade and subject under a country's formal policy.

Editorial explanation

Qualification status is used in teacher-workforce indicators and helps distinguish staffing shortages from shortages of personnel who meet formal teaching requirements.
