Learning Sciences / Concept

Social learning

Definition

Learning that develops through interaction with other people and communities, including observation, conversation, collaboration, participation, and shared sensemaking.

Editorial explanation

Social learning recognizes that knowledge and capability often develop through networks and relationships rather than only through formal instruction.
