Data availability

Definition

The condition in which data exists and can be accessed or retrieved, regardless of whether it has been officially reported or whether its quality is sufficient for a particular use.

Editorial explanation

Separating availability from reporting helps diagnose whether a data problem is caused by missing collection, limited access, weak quality, or failure to publish and share existing data.
