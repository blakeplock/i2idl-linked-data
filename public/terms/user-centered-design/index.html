Human-Centered Design / Method

User-centered design

Definition

A design approach that begins with the needs, contexts, and experiences of end users and stakeholders and uses that understanding to shape flexible, inclusive solutions.

Editorial explanation

For EdTech, user-centered design supports equity, adaptability, and sustainability by accounting for differences in gender, disability, language, culture, and context.
