Activity Profile Resource

Definition

The xAPI resource at /activities/profile for storing and retrieving generic profile documents associated with an Activity.

Continue exploring

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Editorial explanation

Activity Profile documents let applications attach durable, application-specific information to an Activity without changing the Activity identifier or Statement model.
