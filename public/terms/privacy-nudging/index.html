Human-Centered Design / Method

Privacy nudging

Definition

The use of interface cues, defaults, explanations, or other design interventions to help users make more informed privacy choices.

Continue exploring

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Open this concept in the glossary →

Editorial explanation

Privacy decisions are complex; carefully designed nudges can support better choices without requiring users to become privacy experts.
