Data culture

Definition

An organizational and institutional environment in which transparency, collaboration, participation, openness, shared definitions, and the regular use of evidence support decision-making.

Editorial explanation

A data culture affects whether investments in data systems translate into routine use, shared understanding, accountability, and policy action.
