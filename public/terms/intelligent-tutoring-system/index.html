Intelligent tutoring system

Definition

A computer-based instructional system that uses automated procedures to guide learners through problems, provide hints, deliver feedback, and adapt support based on learner performance.

Editorial explanation

Intelligent tutors were an important step beyond fixed branching instruction and remain a major foundation for adaptive learning systems.
