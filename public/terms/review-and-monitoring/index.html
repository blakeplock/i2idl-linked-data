Review and monitoring

Definition

The systematic publication, examination, and tracking of education information against plans, indicators, targets, and benchmarks to support transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement.

Editorial explanation

Review and monitoring closes the loop between data production and governance by making progress visible and enabling corrective action when results diverge from goals.
