Learning Sciences / Concept

Locus of control

Definition

A belief about whether outcomes are primarily influenced by one's own actions or by forces outside one's control.

Editorial explanation

An internal locus of control is discussed as one factor that can support confidence, self-efficacy, persistence, and self-regulated learning.
