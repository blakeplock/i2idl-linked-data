xAPI Pattern

Definition

A description of groups of xAPI Statements that match particular Statement Templates and are arranged according to specified ordering structures.

Editorial explanation

Patterns let a Profile describe how multiple Statements relate over time, such as the sequence of interactions that make up a larger learning or performance process.
