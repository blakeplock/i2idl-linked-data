Learning Sciences / Practice

Metacognitive monitoring

Definition

The process of observing and judging one's own understanding, performance, strategies, and progress while learning or performing a task.

Editorial explanation

Accurate self-monitoring helps learners decide when to change strategies, seek help, practice more, or adjust goals.
