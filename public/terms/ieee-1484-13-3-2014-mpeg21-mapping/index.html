IEEE 1484.13.3-2014 — MPEG-21 Digital Item Declaration Mapping

Definition

An IEEE recommended practice mapping elements and attributes from the MPEG-21 Digital Item Declaration model to the IEEE conceptual model for aggregating learning, education, and training resources.

Editorial explanation

Mappings between resource models make it easier for learning systems to interpret and exchange packaged or aggregated digital resources created under different technical specifications.
