Learning Engineering / Method

Exploratory research

Definition

Research organized to investigate open questions, emerging possibilities, and uncertain design spaces where questions, methods, or outcomes may evolve as evidence is gathered.

Editorial explanation

In fast-changing technology contexts, exploratory research creates room to discover uses, risks, and design directions that cannot be specified fully in advance.
