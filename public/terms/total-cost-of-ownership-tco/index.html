Total cost of ownership (TCO)

Definition

The full lifecycle cost of acquiring, integrating, operating, supporting, changing, and retiring a technology or system.

Editorial explanation

Learning infrastructure decisions can create long-term costs through integration, data migration, licensing, maintenance, and dependence on specialized expertise.
