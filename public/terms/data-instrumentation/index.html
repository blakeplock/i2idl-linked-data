Data & Instrumentation / Practice

Data instrumentation

Definition

The deliberate design and implementation of mechanisms that capture events, states, context, and other data needed to understand a learning system.

Editorial explanation

If the right evidence is not instrumented at the source, later analytics cannot reconstruct it reliably.
