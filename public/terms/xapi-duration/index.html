xAPI duration

Definition

A duration value used in xAPI to represent elapsed time for an experience or result, formatted according to the ISO 8601 duration rules referenced by the standard.

Editorial explanation

Standardized duration values allow systems to exchange elapsed-time data without inventing incompatible local formats.
