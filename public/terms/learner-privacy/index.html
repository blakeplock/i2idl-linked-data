Learner privacy

Definition

The protection and management of privacy interests associated with the collection, modeling, sharing, storage, and use of data about learners and their activities.

Editorial explanation

Digital learning systems can observe detailed behavior across contexts, so privacy affects trust, adoption, personalization, and the legitimacy of data use.
