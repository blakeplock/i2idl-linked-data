SubStatement

Definition

An xAPI Statement-like Object embedded inside another Statement. It contains an Actor, Verb, and Object and may include Result, Context, Timestamp, and Attachments, but cannot itself contain another SubStatement.

Editorial explanation

SubStatements let a Statement describe another structured experience as its Object while preventing unbounded nesting.
