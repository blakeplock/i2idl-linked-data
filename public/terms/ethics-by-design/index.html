Ethics by design

Definition

An approach in which ethical requirements are intentionally built into the design and validation of AI systems rather than addressed only after deployment.

Editorial explanation

Embedding ethical requirements before adoption can reduce risks to human rights, safety, privacy, inclusion, and accountability in educational settings.
