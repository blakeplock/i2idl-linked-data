IEEE 1484.2-2024 — Learning and Employment Record Ecosystems

Definition

An IEEE recommended practice for ecosystem-scale systems that issue, hold, present, and exchange Learning and Employment Records (LERs) representing education, skills, employment, and related achievements using interoperable digital credentials and shared services.

Editorial explanation

LER ecosystems connect education and employment data across organizational boundaries, supporting portable records, skills-based hiring, career mobility, and learner-controlled exchange of verified achievements.
