Human-Centered Design / Practice

Experience design

Definition

A design practice focused on shaping the sensory, emotional, cognitive, behavioral, and social qualities of an experience so it becomes more meaningful and memorable.

Editorial explanation

Learning experience design can borrow experience-design methods to account for motivation, emotion, context, identity, and social connection in addition to cognition.
