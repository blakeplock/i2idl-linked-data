Maturity model

Definition

A staged framework used to assess current capability and guide incremental improvement over time rather than treating compliance with the highest level as the sole objective.

Editorial explanation

Maturity models provide a structured way to identify current strengths and weaknesses, prioritize investment, and plan progressive capability development.
