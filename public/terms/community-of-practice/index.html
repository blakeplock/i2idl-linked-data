Learning Sciences / Concept

Community of practice

Definition

A community in which people learn through social participation in a shared practice, developing knowledge through interaction with others engaged in related work or activity.

Editorial explanation

Communities of practice situate learning in participation, relationships, and authentic practice rather than treating knowledge as isolated information.
