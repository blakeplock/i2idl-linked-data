Human-controlled AI in education

Definition

The principle that the selection, use, monitoring, and consequential decisions involving AI remain under meaningful human control and human accountability.

Editorial explanation

Human control helps prevent AI systems from displacing legitimate educational judgement or obscuring responsibility for decisions affecting learners.
