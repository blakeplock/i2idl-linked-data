Learning Engineering / Concept

Synchronous learning

Definition

Learning activity that occurs in real time, with participants engaging at the same time and often together in a shared physical or virtual setting.

Editorial explanation

Synchronous formats can support immediate interaction, discussion, guidance, and feedback, but they also impose scheduling and connectivity requirements.
