Learning Engineering / Practice

Stakeholder engagement

Definition

The practice of involving people who are affected by, responsible for, or able to influence a learning challenge or solution.

Editorial explanation

Stakeholder input helps teams understand constraints, needs, incentives, and consequences that may not be visible from the learner perspective alone.
