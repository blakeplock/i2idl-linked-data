Learning Engineering / Practice

Challenge identification

Definition

The stage of learning engineering in which a team frames the problem, context, stakeholders, constraints, and evidence before designing a solution.

Editorial explanation

Clear problem framing prevents teams from optimizing the wrong thing or treating a symptom as the underlying learning challenge.
