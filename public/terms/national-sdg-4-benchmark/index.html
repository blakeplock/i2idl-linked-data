National SDG 4 benchmark

Definition

A nationally established quantitative reference point used to translate global SDG 4 ambitions into context-sensitive targets for monitoring progress, accountability, planning, and policy dialogue.

Editorial explanation

National benchmarks connect global education goals with country starting points, national plans, intermediate targets, and public commitments.
