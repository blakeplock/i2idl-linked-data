Learning Sciences / Concept

Meaningful learning

Definition

Learning characterized by intentional, active, constructive, authentic, and cooperative engagement that helps learners connect experiences and build usable understanding.

Editorial explanation

The framework provides a way to design learning for deeper understanding and transfer across varied contexts rather than for passive content exposure.
