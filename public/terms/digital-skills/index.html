Education / Concept

Digital skills

Definition

Skills for using digital technologies, ranging from basic technology competencies to higher-order capabilities such as coding, computational thinking, and ethics.

Editorial explanation

Access alone does not produce effective participation in digital learning. Learners and educators also need skills that let them use technology productively and responsibly.
