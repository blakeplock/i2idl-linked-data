Learning Sciences / Practice

Self-monitoring

Definition

A learner’s ongoing observation of their own time, tasks, understanding, stress, progress, and learning behavior in order to decide when adjustments are needed.

Editorial explanation

Self-monitoring helps learners notice gaps in understanding, manage effort and time, seek help, and change strategies before problems compound.
