Profile-based Statement conformance

Definition

Conformance in which Statements follow the matching Statement Templates and Patterns described by a Profile version, with the Profile version identified as a category context activity when authored by a conformant Learning Record Provider.

Editorial explanation

This connects a Statement to explicit, reusable domain rules and makes stronger semantic and structural interoperability possible across Learning Record Providers and validators.
