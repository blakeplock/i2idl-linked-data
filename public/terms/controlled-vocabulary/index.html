Controlled vocabulary

Definition

A managed set of defined terms and identifiers used consistently within a domain so that people and machines can refer to the same concepts in the same way.

Editorial explanation

One of the explicit goals of xAPI Profiles is to provide a common way to express controlled vocabularies for reusable xAPI concepts.
