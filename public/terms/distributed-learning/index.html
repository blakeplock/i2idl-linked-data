Learning Engineering / Concept

Distributed learning

Definition

Learning delivered beyond a single physical classroom through distributed technologies and environments, including distance education, online learning, simulations, mobile learning, and other network-enabled experiences.

Editorial explanation

The term connects the history of distance education with today's broader ecosystem of digital, mobile, simulated, and networked learning.
