Learning Sciences / Concept

Cognitive load

Definition

The mental effort required to process information in working memory during a task.

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Editorial explanation

Learning experiences can fail when unnecessary complexity consumes limited cognitive capacity that should be available for learning.
