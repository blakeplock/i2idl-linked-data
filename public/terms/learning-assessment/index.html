Assessment & Measurement / Method

Learning assessment

Definition

A structured process for measuring learners’ knowledge and skills in specified subject areas at particular ages or grade levels, often to estimate learning outcomes for a wider education system.

Editorial explanation

Learning assessments provide evidence about what students know and can do and can support analysis of learning quality, equity, and system performance.
