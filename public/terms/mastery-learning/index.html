Learning Sciences / Practice

Mastery learning

Definition

An instructional approach that expects learners to demonstrate a defined level of proficiency before moving on to more advanced material or competencies.

Editorial explanation

Mastery shifts progression from seat time toward evidence of capability and is a recurring foundation for adaptive and competency-based approaches.
