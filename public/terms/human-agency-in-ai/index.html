Human agency in AI

Definition

The capacity of people to retain meaningful autonomy, judgement, and control when evaluating and using AI systems rather than allowing AI to displace human decision-making.

Editorial explanation

Protecting human agency is especially important in education because AI can influence teaching, assessment, knowledge construction, and learner autonomy.
