Context Activities

Definition

Activity references included in an xAPI Statement Context to express how the recorded experience relates to broader or associated Activities.

Continue exploring

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Editorial explanation

Context Activities make it possible to connect a fine-grained event to a course, program, grouping, category, or other surrounding experience without changing the Statement Object.
