Culture change

Definition

The deliberate effort to shift shared assumptions, behaviors, norms, incentives, and ways of working so an organization can adopt and sustain a new model of learning.

Editorial explanation

Technology and policy changes can fail when organizational culture continues to reward old behaviors or resists experimentation and new forms of collaboration.
