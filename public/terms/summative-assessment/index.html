Assessment & Measurement / Practice

Summative assessment

Definition

Assessment used to judge or document learning at the end of a defined period, course, or instructional sequence.

Editorial explanation

Summative assessment is useful for certification and reporting, but by itself provides limited support for continuous adjustment during learning.
