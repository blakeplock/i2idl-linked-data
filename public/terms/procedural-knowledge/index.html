Learning Sciences / Concept

Procedural knowledge

Definition

Knowledge of how to carry out a procedure, perform a task, or solve a problem.

Editorial explanation

Learners need opportunities to connect procedural knowledge with conceptual understanding so they can use a procedure flexibly rather than only repeat memorized steps.
