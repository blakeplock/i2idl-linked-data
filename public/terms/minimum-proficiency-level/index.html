Minimum Proficiency Level

Definition

A benchmark representing the basic knowledge or skill expected in a learning domain, such as reading or mathematics, at a given age or grade.

Editorial explanation

A common proficiency threshold supports interpretation and comparison of learning-assessment results across assessments and education systems.
