Human-Centered Design / Practice

Accessibility

Definition

The practice of designing learning technologies, content, and interactions so people with a wide range of abilities can perceive, operate, understand, and use them.

Editorial explanation

Accessibility broadens participation and is part of evaluating whether a learning solution works for the people it is meant to serve.
