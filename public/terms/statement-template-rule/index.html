Statement Template rule

Definition

A restriction in a Statement Template that identifies one or more Statement locations with JSONPath and constrains the values or presence allowed there.

Editorial explanation

Template rules provide the fine-grained mechanism for expressing what a conformant Statement must include, exclude, or restrict.
