Learning Sciences / Concept

Building expertise

Definition

The development of increasingly organized, retrievable, and usable knowledge and skill within a domain through experience and practice.

Editorial explanation

Expertise changes how learners perceive problems, use prior knowledge, and allocate attention, which affects how instruction should be designed.
