Privacy by design

Definition

An approach in which privacy protections are incorporated into a system's architecture and development process from the beginning rather than added after deployment.

Editorial explanation

It helps learning systems avoid privacy becoming an afterthought represented only by complicated settings or policies that users cannot realistically manage.
