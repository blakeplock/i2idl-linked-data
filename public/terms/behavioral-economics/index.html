Human-Centered Design / Discipline

Behavioral economics

Definition

A field that studies systematic biases, heuristics, and decision patterns that cause people to make choices that depart from purely rational models.

Editorial explanation

Learning designers can use behavioral economics to account for real-world decision behavior instead of assuming learners will always act rationally on information.
