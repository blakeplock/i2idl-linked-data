Learning Engineering / Concept

Online learning modality

Definition

A mode of online or remote learning that can be described along dimensions such as synchronous versus asynchronous participation and instructor-facilitated versus resource-facilitated activity.

Editorial explanation

Choosing a modality changes how learners interact with content, instructors, peers, time, and technology, so modality should follow the learning objective and learner context.
