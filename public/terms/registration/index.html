Registration

Definition

An identifier for an instance of an Actor experiencing a particular Activity.

Continue exploring

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Open this concept in the glossary →

Editorial explanation

Registrations help distinguish separate instances or runs of an experience and can be used to group related Statements without assuming that one Activity or Actor uniquely defines a session.
