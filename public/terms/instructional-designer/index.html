Learning Engineering / Discipline

Instructional designer

Definition

A professional who analyzes learning needs and designs, develops, implements, and evaluates instruction, learning experiences, and supporting materials or systems.

Editorial explanation

Instructional designers remain central to modern learning teams, while their role increasingly intersects with technology, data, systems, and multidisciplinary collaboration.
