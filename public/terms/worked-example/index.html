Learning Sciences / Method

Worked example

Definition

A step-by-step demonstration of how to perform a task or solve a problem, used to show learners how a complete solution is constructed.

Editorial explanation

Worked examples can reduce unnecessary problem-solving demands early in learning and can be faded as learners become more capable of completing steps independently.
