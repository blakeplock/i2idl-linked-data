xAPI persona

Definition

A set of one or more identity representations that uniquely describes an Actor in a particular context. The standard distinguishes personas from the broader real-world person.

Editorial explanation

The persona model recognizes that the same person may be represented differently across contexts or systems, which affects how records can safely be correlated.
