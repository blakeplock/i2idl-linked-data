Learning Sciences / Concept

Motivation

Definition

The processes that influence whether a learner initiates, directs, persists in, and values an activity.

Editorial explanation

Motivation interacts with difficulty, context, goals, identity, feedback, and perceived competence, so it is a design variable as well as a learner characteristic.
