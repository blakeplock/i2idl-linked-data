Data stewardship

Definition

The organizational responsibility for governing how data is held, protected, accessed, shared, maintained, and used across its lifecycle.

Editorial explanation

Learning ecosystems cross organizational boundaries, so responsible data use depends on clear stewardship roles as well as technical interoperability.
