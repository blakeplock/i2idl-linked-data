Learning Sciences / Concept

Social constructivism

Definition

A view of learning that emphasizes the role of interaction, language, culture, and shared activity in the construction of knowledge.

Editorial explanation

It highlights that learning can be shaped by communities and social contexts rather than only by individual cognition.
