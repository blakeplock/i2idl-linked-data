xAPI Agent

Definition

An xAPI representation of an individual Actor, identified through one of the identity properties defined by the standard and optionally accompanied by a name.

Editorial explanation

Agents are the basic individual identity object in xAPI and are used as Actors, Objects, Authorities, and contextual participants.
