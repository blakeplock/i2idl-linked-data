Assessment & Measurement / Concept

Asset model

Definition

An approach that begins with the capabilities and strengths a learner already possesses and focuses on adding new capabilities rather than primarily diagnosing deficits against group averages.

Editorial explanation

An asset model supports individualized growth, lifelong learning, and recognition of strengths that conventional norm-referenced systems may overlook.
