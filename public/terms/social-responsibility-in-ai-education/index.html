Social responsibility in AI education

Definition

The responsibility to consider and act on the wider effects of AI on human welfare, inclusion, social justice, civic life, and the environment.

Editorial explanation

AI use in education has consequences beyond individual classrooms, including effects on social norms, rights, equity, and planetary well-being.
