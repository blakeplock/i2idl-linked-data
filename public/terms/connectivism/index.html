Learning Sciences / Concept

Connectivism

Definition

A learning theory that treats knowledge as distributed across networks of connections and emphasizes continual learning, current information, and the ability to recognize connections among sources and fields.

Editorial explanation

Connectivism provides a conceptual basis for networked learning environments where knowledge is not confined to one instructor, course, or institution.
