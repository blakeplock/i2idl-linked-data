IEEE 2955-2025 — Self-Learning Tutorials and Side-by-Side Learning

Definition

An IEEE recommended practice describing methods for creating self-learning tutorials, including screencast-based and spoken tutorials, with guidance for side-by-side learning techniques.

Editorial explanation

It gives tutorial creators a common, standards-based methodology for designing instructional experiences that learners can follow independently while working alongside the demonstrated task.
