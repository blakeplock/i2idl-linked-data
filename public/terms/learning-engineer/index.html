Learning Engineering / Discipline

Learning engineer

Definition

A professional who applies evidence about human learning and development at scale to create reliable, affordable, data-rich learning environments within real contexts.

Editorial explanation

The role reflects the move from isolated course design toward multidisciplinary, evidence-driven design and operation of complex learning systems.
