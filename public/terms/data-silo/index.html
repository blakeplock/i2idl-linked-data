Data silo

Definition

An isolated store of data that does not readily exchange information with other applications, systems, or organizational data sources.

Explore related concepts, research, and semantic relationships in the full I2IDL Glossary of Digital Learning.

Editorial explanation

Data silos make it harder to combine information across an education ecosystem and limit the value of data for coordinated decisions and services.
